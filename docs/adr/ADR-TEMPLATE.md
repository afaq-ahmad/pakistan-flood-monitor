# ADR-NNN: Decision title

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Decision owners:** Roles or teams accountable for the decision
- **Related work:** Issue, pull request, prompt, incident, or prior ADR links

## Context

State the decision pressure, current repository evidence, operational/scientific problem, constraints,
and what is explicitly out of scope. Distinguish facts from assumptions.

## Decision drivers

- Scientific validity, including CRS/units, time semantics, provenance, quality, and uncertainty
- Operational safety, availability, security/privacy, and authority boundaries
- Compatibility, migration cost, maintainability, performance, and free/open-data constraints

## Considered options

### Option A: Name

Describe the approach, evidence, benefits, costs, failure modes, and scientific limitations.

### Option B: Name

Describe the approach, evidence, benefits, costs, failure modes, and scientific limitations.

## Decision

State the chosen option and the normative rules future implementations must follow. Do not describe a
capability as operational without acceptance evidence.

## Consequences

List positive consequences, trade-offs, new risks, data/product labeling effects, and follow-up work.

## Compatibility, migration, and rollback

Describe affected packages, APIs, data/schema contracts, compatibility window, migration sequencing,
rollback path, and how duplicate/deprecated behavior will be retired.

## Validation and acceptance evidence

Define the tests, benchmarks, data-quality checks, review/authority approval, and operational evidence
required to accept the decision and any later claim of maturity.

## Unresolved questions

List decisions deliberately deferred to later ADRs or scoped tasks.
