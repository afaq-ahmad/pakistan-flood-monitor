## Practical benchmark-to-repo interpretation
- The repository is **architecturally aligned** with many benchmark ideas, but several benchmark-critical capabilities are currently prototype-level, partially implemented, or not yet verifiable in canonical runtime.
- Highest benchmark gaps affecting launch credibility:
  1. authoritative and durable event/audit persistence,
  2. scene-derived and validated SAR science in canonical flow,
  3. agency-ready dissemination/governance templates,
  4. standards-grade interoperability packaging (OGC/STAC/COG/GeoParquet/vector tiles).
