# Pakistan flood information: user needs and evidence

Research checked 2026-11-18; these are historical findings, not current conditions.

- **Residents and community relays:** The World Bank's 2026 account of a 1,207-household survey
  in Buner, Shangla and Swat reports 85% received no warning and half of those warned had under
  30 minutes. Mosque announcements, neighbours and phone calls were important last-mile channels.
  This sample describes affected districts, not all Pakistan. [World Bank, 3 September 2026](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/09/03/preparing-better-for-monsoons-lessons-from-the-2025-flash-floods-in-pakistan).
- **Isolated households:** [Dawn, 3 September 2025](https://www.dawn.com/news/1939265/crisis-in-the-making-complaints-of-missing-facilities-emerge-from-punjabs-flood-hit-areas)
  describes reports of missing facilities in flood-affected Punjab. Resource directories require
  verification and expiry; no road, route or shelter availability should be inferred.
- **Farmers and livestock owners:** Reports describe losses to crops and rural livelihoods:
  [Dawn, 12 September 2025: Ravi flooding](https://www.dawn.com/news/1941317/residents-share-their-losses-during-ravi-flooding)
  and [Dawn, 28 September 2025: Punjab farmers](https://www.dawn.com/news/1945132/punjab-floods-small-farmers-contractors-face-ruin).
  Separate estimated exposure, reported loss and verified damage.
- **People needing health, water or shelter:** [IFRC 2025 emergency page](https://www.ifrc.org/emergency/pakistan-monsoon-floods-2025)
  reports shortages and disease risk. Avoid collecting identifying or precise location data without
  a reviewed protection and retention policy.

## Existing systems and boundaries

[PMD Flood Forecasting Division](https://ffd.weather.gov.pk/flood-ews) publishes information for
riverine, flash and urban flooding; [NDMA](https://www.ndma.gov.pk/sitreps?cat_id=3) publishes dated
situation reports. [PITB describes Punjab's flood management system](https://pitb.gov.pk/pfdms),
including official coordination and SMS. The Punjab PDMA helpline page supplied in the research timed
out during verification; no number is presented. Links are not evidence of open APIs, licensing,
current data, accessibility or partnerships. Review terms before automated ingestion.

Mountain flash floods, riverine floods, hill torrents, urban inundation and GLOF have different
spatial and temporal behaviour. The project must not imply coverage without appropriate data and
validation. An empty map is not safety evidence. Local-only CSV and independent exposure paths
remain an open P0-003 risk in the engineering plan. Public latest-run/advisory views are limited;
operational observations and reviewed products are incomplete.
